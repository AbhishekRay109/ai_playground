# Acme Corp Travel and Expense Policy

Version 3.2, effective 1 March 2025. Owner: Finance Operations.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees and contractors who travel on behalf of Acme Corp.

## 2. Booking rules

All business travel must be booked through the Acme travel portal. Domestic trips must be booked at least 7 days in advance. International travel must be booked at least 21 days in advance and requires written approval from a department director.

Economy class is the default for all flights. Business class is permitted only for flights longer than 8 hours of continuous flying time, or when a medical certificate requires it.

## 3. Accommodation

The nightly hotel limit is 180 USD in standard cities and 260 USD in high-cost cities (London, New York, Zurich, Tokyo, Singapore). Stays above the limit require pre-approval from Finance Operations.

## 4. Meals and per diem

Employees receive a per diem of 65 USD per day for meals during domestic travel and 85 USD per day for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## 5. Exclusions

The following expenses are excluded and will not be reimbursed:
- Personal entertainment, including movies, spa treatments and minibar charges.
- Traffic fines, parking tickets and penalties of any kind.
- Upgrades to business or first class that were not pre-approved.
- Expenses for family members or travel companions.
- Lost luggage or personal items, which are covered by the employee's own insurance.

## 6. Submitting expenses

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of returning from a trip. Receipts are required for every expense above 25 USD. Late submissions after 60 days are rejected unless the CFO grants an exception.

## 7. Travel insurance

Acme provides travel medical insurance for all international business trips. Coverage includes emergency medical treatment up to 250,000 USD and medical evacuation.
