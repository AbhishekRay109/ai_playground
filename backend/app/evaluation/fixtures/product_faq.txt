Acme Gadgets Product FAQ

Q: What is the warranty on the Aurora Laptop?
A: Aurora laptops include a 2-year limited hardware warranty. An extended 3-year AcmeCare plan can be purchased within 60 days of delivery.

Q: How long does the Pulse Smartwatch battery last?
A: Typical battery life is 6 days with normal use and about 30 hours with continuous GPS tracking.

Q: Can I return a product?
A: Unopened products can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Opened products can be returned within 14 days and are subject to a 10 percent restocking fee, except for defective items.

Q: Do Echo Wireless Earbuds support noise cancellation?
A: Yes. Echo Wireless Earbuds feature active noise cancellation with three adjustable levels and a transparency mode.

Q: Which countries do you ship to?
A: We ship to the European Union, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Australia, Singapore and India. Shipping is free for orders above 99 USD.
