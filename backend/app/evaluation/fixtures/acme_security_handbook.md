# Acme Corp Information Security Handbook

Last reviewed: January 2025.

## Passwords and authentication

All accounts must use passwords of at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the VPN and all production systems. Hardware security keys are required for administrators.

Passwords must never be shared, written down, or reused across systems. A password manager approved by IT (currently Bitwarden) must be used.

## Devices

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system security updates must be installed within 14 days of release.

Personal devices may access email only through the managed mobile app and may not store customer data.

## Incident reporting

Suspected security incidents, including phishing emails, lost devices and accidental data disclosure, must be reported to security@acme.example within 1 hour of discovery. Employees must not attempt to investigate incidents on their own.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer personal data is Confidential. Payment card data is Restricted and may only be processed in the PCI environment.

## Acceptable use

Company systems may not be used for cryptocurrency mining, for storing pirated software, or for running unapproved remote-access tools.
